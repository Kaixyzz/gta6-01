**SOLARA BAY — Streets of the Bay** is a top-down 2D open-world crime game set in a sun-soaked coastal city. Steal cars, outrun the police, and work your way up through 6 story missions — from your first stolen wheels to the final showdown as King of the Bay.

- 6 story missions with a wanted level, real police chases, and shootouts
- Side stashes hidden around the beach, park, and marina (+$100 each)
- Vehicle variety: regular cars, a fast motorbike, and a heavy truck — all stealable
- Living city: traffic, pedestrians, a beachfront promenade, park, and marina
- Title screen, pause menu, save/load, minimap, and WebAudio sound — all drawn and synthesized in code, no external assets

## How to run

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory game
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Keyboard + mouse: WASD to move/drive, E to enter/exit cars, mouse to aim, click to shoot, P to pause.
